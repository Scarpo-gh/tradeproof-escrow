# TradeProof demo video plan

**Duration:** 45–60 seconds. **Audience:** Ignyte judges. **Format:** 1280×720 MP4, silent captions (no claims beyond verifiable testnet evidence).

## Narrative arc

1. **Context (0–7s):** Synthetic UAE-to-global SME invoice workflow; Arc Testnet/test-USDC boundary.
2. **MVP (7–18s):** Actual running TradeProof invoice form. It creates a deterministic invoice instruction without wallet fields.
3. **Proof binding (18–28s):** Invoice hash and delivery-proof hash bind the synthetic business instruction to the lifecycle.
4. **Completion path (28–40s):** Job A: create, set budget, fund, submit proof, complete. Display its public Arcscan completion hash.
5. **Refund path (40–52s):** Separate Job B: fund, expire, refund. Display its public Arcscan refund hash after chain verification.
6. **Close (52–58s):** Repository, testnet-only scope, no custody/no mainnet/no real invoices.

## Visual language

Dark navy background, cyan for the UI/API, violet for hashes/evidence, amber for Arc Testnet, green for terminal state. Captions remain on screen at least 4 seconds and use a 28px+ sans-serif font.
